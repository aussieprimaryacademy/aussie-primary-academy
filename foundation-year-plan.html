Foundation Full-Year Learning Plan

A four-term pathway through selected APA Maths, English / Phonics and Science resources. Start with the term that matches the learner’s current skills and adjust the pace as needed.

How the year is organised

  • Term 1: The existing 10-week Foundation plan introduces key number, early literacy and Science topics.
  • Term 2: Build confidence through further number, shape, measurement, phonics, early reading and guided exploration.
  • Term 3: Extend familiar skills with more independent practice, connected reading and writing, data and practical Science.
  • Term 4: Consolidate Foundation learning and build confidence for the transition to Year 1.

Each term is a suggested practice sequence, not a complete school program. Use teacher judgement and the official curriculum to determine the full teaching scope.

Scope and curriculum notes

This APA pathway covers selected Foundation Maths, English / Phonics and Science resources. HASS is not included in this programme. Fractions and decimals are not part of this Foundation plan.

Curriculum codes are not invented or added at term level. Check each individual worksheet page for its stated curriculum connection and learning intention, and verify against the official Australian Curriculum.

Some existing worksheets have audit items or are still awaiting full visual review. A link in a learning plan does not itself mean that a PDF has passed every content and visual quality check. Where a worksheet needs correction, use a teacher-led practical activity until the resource is corrected.

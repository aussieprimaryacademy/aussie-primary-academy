About Aussie Primary Academy

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent educational project providing free, printable learning resources for Australian primary students from Foundation to Year 6.

Our Story

Aussie Primary Academy was created to make useful, curriculum-aware learning practice easier to access. Many families and educators spend time searching for clear, printable activities that match Australian primary learning needs — without expensive subscriptions or complicated sign-ups.

This project exists to reduce that friction. We focus on practical worksheets and simple parent guides that support focused practice at home, in classrooms and during tutoring sessions.

What We Focus On

Clear priorities that guide every resource we publish.

  • Free Access

    No Barriers

    Worksheets are free to download. No account is required to access the core resources.

  • Australian Context

    Curriculum-Aware

    Resources are developed with reference to the Australian Curriculum V9.0 and the needs of Australian primary learners.

  • Practical Use

    Ready to Print

    Activities are designed with clear instructions and answer support where appropriate, so they can be used immediately.

Who These Resources Are For

Our worksheets and guides are created for:

  • Australian parents supporting learning at home
  • Classroom teachers looking for focused practice materials
  • Tutors working with primary students
  • Homeschool families

We cover Foundation through Year 6 across Maths, English, Reading, Writing, Phonics and Science.

How We Work

Every resource is created with a practical goal: help a child practise a clear skill. We prioritise clarity over complexity, and usefulness over volume.

We regularly review feedback and improve resources where needed. If you notice an error or have a suggestion, you can reach us through the Contact page.

Important Note

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent educational publisher. Our resources are Australian Curriculum-aligned and ACARA-aware, but they are not official ACARA products and are not endorsed by ACARA.

Any “NAPLAN-style” materials are independent practice resources only. They are not official NAPLAN tests or publications.

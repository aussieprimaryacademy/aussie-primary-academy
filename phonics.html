Key phonics skills

  • Recognising and matching letters with their sounds
  • Blending sounds to read words and segmenting words for spelling
  • Reading and spelling CVC words, blends and digraphs
  • Noticing short-vowel patterns, rhyming words and syllables
  • Applying phonics knowledge when reading decodable words and sentences

Phonics is one part of learning to read. Combine sound-based practice with meaningful reading, vocabulary development and comprehension.

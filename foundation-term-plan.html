Foundation 10-Week Learning Plan

Use this flexible 10-week Foundation learning plan with free Maths, English and Science worksheets for Australian families, teachers and homeschoolers.

Start here

  • Begin at Week 1 if the skills are new, or choose the week that matches what your child is ready to practise.
  • Complete one Maths, one English and one Science activity each week. Allow about 15–20 minutes for each activity; split a session if your child needs a break.
  • Print the worksheet, talk through one example, let your child try, then discuss the answers together.

How to use the plan

  • Keep the sessions short and positive. Stop when your child becomes tired rather than rushing to finish.
  • If a skill is difficult, revisit the previous week or repeat the same worksheet with adult guidance.
  • If the activity is easy, ask your child to explain their strategy, give another example or apply the idea to an everyday situation.

Your 10-week practice plan

Three short activities each week: Maths, English and Science.

  • Week 1

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 1

    Maths Numbers 0–20: name, represent and order — Recognise, represent and order numbers to 20. (View worksheet)

    English Lowercase letter formation — Practise forming lowercase letters clearly. (View worksheet)

    Science Features of plants — Observe and describe familiar plant features. (View worksheet)

  • Week 2

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 2

    Maths Subitising: how many? — Recognise small quantities without counting one by one. (View worksheet)

    English Rhyme, retell and speaking — Notice rhyme and practise retelling ideas aloud. (View worksheet)

    Science Features of animals — Observe and describe familiar animal features. (View worksheet)

  • Week 3

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 3

    Maths Counting and comparing collections to 20 — Count collections and compare how many. (View worksheet)

    English Beginning sounds — Hear and identify the first sounds in words. (View worksheet)

    Science Grouping living things — Sort living things using observable features. (View worksheet)

  • Week 4

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 4

    Maths Part-part-whole: making 10 — Explore how two parts combine to make a whole of 10. (View worksheet)

    English Blending and segmenting CVC words — Blend sounds to read and segment simple CVC words. (View worksheet)

    Science How objects move — Explore how size, shape and material can affect movement. (View worksheet)

  • Week 5

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 5

    Maths Adding and taking away to 10 — Solve simple addition and subtraction tasks to 10. (View worksheet)

    English Reading comprehension: simple sentences — Read short sentences and show understanding. (View worksheet)

    Science Materials and their properties — Describe observable properties of everyday materials. (View worksheet)

    Family tip: Let a younger sibling talk or draw while the Year 3 learner records a more detailed observation.

  • Week 6

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 6

    Maths Equal sharing and grouping — Share small collections fairly and notice equal groups. (View worksheet)

    English A Hat for Sam: decodable reading — Apply letter-sound knowledge when reading a decodable text. (View worksheet)

    Science Revisit plant features — Compare two plants and explain one similarity and one difference. (View worksheet)

  • Week 7

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 7

    Maths Repeating patterns — Copy, continue and describe repeating patterns. (View worksheet)

    English Sentence writing: capital letters and full stops — Write simple sentences with a capital letter and full stop. (View worksheet)

    Science Revisit grouping living things — Explain why familiar living things belong in a group. (View worksheet)

  • Week 8

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 8

    Maths Sorting, naming and creating shapes — Name and sort familiar two-dimensional shapes. (View worksheet)

    English Text forms and purposes — Notice that different texts are created for different purposes. (View worksheet)

    Science Revisit how objects move — Predict how a change in shape or surface may affect movement. (View worksheet)

  • Week 9

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 9

    Maths Length, mass, capacity and duration — Compare and describe everyday measurements. (View worksheet)

    English Listening and speaking — Listen, take turns and share ideas clearly. (View worksheet)

    Science Revisit materials and properties — Compare two materials and explain which is suitable for a simple purpose. (View worksheet)

  • Week 10

    Suggested weekly practice · 15–20 minutes per activity

    Week 10

    Maths My school day: sequencing familiar times — Sequence familiar events across the day. (View worksheet)

    English Literature connections and features — Talk about features and ideas in a familiar literary text. (View worksheet)

    Science Revisit animal features — Choose an animal feature and explain how it helps the animal. (View worksheet)

Multi-age family tip

Use the same everyday materials for different ages. A younger child can sort, count or describe objects aloud while an older sibling records totals, writes labels or explains a pattern. Give each child a task that matches their current learning.

Keep the plan flexible

This is a suggested practice sequence, not a complete school program or a fixed curriculum scope and sequence. Teachers and families should adjust the order, pace and level of support to suit the learner and their local learning program. See the Australian Curriculum planning guidance.

Some Science worksheets are intentionally revisited later in the plan so children can practise a familiar skill with a new question or observation.

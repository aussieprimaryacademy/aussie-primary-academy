Doubling Rule: -ing, -ed

Practise the doubling rule when adding -ing and -ed.

Learning Intention

Students will apply the doubling rule when adding -ing and -ed to short-vowel base words.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Year 4 English literacy and spelling learning.

Teacher Notes

Use CVC examples (run → running, hop → hopped). Contrast with words that do not double.

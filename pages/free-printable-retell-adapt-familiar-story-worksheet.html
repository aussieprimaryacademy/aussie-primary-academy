About this worksheet

This free printable gives learners a focused opportunity to practise retelling story events and trying a creative change. It can be used during a class lesson, in a small-group session or at home with an adult.

What students can practise

  • Recall key events in sequence
  • Describe an important character or event
  • Suggest a change while keeping the story understandable

Read the instructions together first if the student needs support. Encourage them to explain their thinking rather than rushing to finish.

Parent and teacher tip

Choose a story the child already knows. Have them retell the main events orally before writing, then ask how one change might affect what happens next.

Make the learning stick

After completing the worksheet, ask the student to explain one answer in their own words. If they are unsure, model one example, work through another together, and then let them try independently.

Choose the next activity based on what the student can already do confidently and where they need more practice.

Counting Collections to 20

Count and compare collections of objects up to 20.

Learning Intention

Students count and compare collections of objects up to 20 and explain their thinking.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Foundation Maths learning. (AC9MFN03)

Teacher Notes

Practise counting real collections together, then talk about which group has more or fewer, before independent work.

Skills Practised

  • Counting collections to 20
  • Comparing groups (more, fewer, > and <)
  • Recording numbers

About This Worksheet

This Foundation maths worksheet supports early number understanding through counting, comparing and recording collections of up to 20 objects. It can be used for independent practice, revision or a short teacher-led activity.

I Can Checklist

  • I can write the number for a collection.
  • I can count a collection carefully.
  • I can say which group has more or fewer.

How to Use It

Before starting, count a small collection of objects together and touch each object once. Ask children to explain how they know how many objects there are, then connect the quantity to its numeral.

Parent Tip

Practise counting during everyday activities such as setting the table, sorting toys or counting steps. Keep the activity playful and ask your child to predict the next number.

Learning Intention

Students will explore feeding relationships and the roles of producers, consumers and decomposers.

Australian Curriculum

AC9S4U01

Living things can be grouped based on their features and roles in food chains and ecosystems.

Teacher Notes

Use local Australian examples (eucalyptus, kangaroo, fungi). Build simple food chains and webs together.

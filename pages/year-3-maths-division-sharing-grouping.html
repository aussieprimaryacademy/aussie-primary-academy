About this worksheet

This Year 3 Maths worksheet helps students divide a collection by sharing equally or making equal groups, then represent the division with a diagram and number sentence. Both approaches are shown clearly with worked examples.

The worksheet is ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

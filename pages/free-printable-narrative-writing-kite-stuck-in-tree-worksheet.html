About this worksheet

This Year 2 narrative writing worksheet centres on the story situation of a kite stuck in a tree. Students can use the prompt to imagine what happens, who is involved and how the problem might be resolved.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 2 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Developing a story idea, sequencing events and communicating a problem and resolution.

What students practise

Before writing, ask the learner what might have caused the kite to get stuck and what could happen next. Encourage an original response and use the worksheet's own instructions to guide the task.

How to use it

Print the PDF and discuss the story situation without supplying a complete story. After writing, ask the learner to retell the events and check whether the reader can follow what happened.

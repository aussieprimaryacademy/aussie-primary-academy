Force: Strength and Direction

Explore how the strength and direction of a push or pull can affect movement. Use this resource with observation, discussion and safe hands-on learning.

Year 1ScienceFree PDF

Learning focus

Explore how the strength and direction of a push or pull can affect movement.

Teacher guidance

Use safe classroom materials such as toy cars or soft balls. Change one feature at a time and ask students to describe how the movement changes. Keep the activity controlled and supervised.

Try it at home

Use a toy car on a clear, safe surface. Compare a gentle push with a stronger push and describe what changes.

Support and extension

Begin with one object and one simple push. Extend by changing direction and asking students to predict the movement first.

How Images Contribute to Meaning

Explore how pictures and other visual features help readers understand a text. Use this printable in class, tutoring or at home with discussion and supported practice.

Year 1EnglishFree PDF

Learning focus

Explore how pictures and other visual features help readers understand a text.

Teacher guidance

Before reading, look closely at the images and ask what information they add. During reading, compare what the words say with what the image shows. Encourage students to point to visual evidence when explaining an idea.

Try it at home

Look at a picture book, poster or information page together. Ask what the image helps you understand that the words do not say directly.

Support and extension

Start by discussing one clear image. Extend by asking students to explain how changing or removing the image could affect the message.

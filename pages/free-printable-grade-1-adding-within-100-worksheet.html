About This Grade 1 Math Worksheet

This Grade 1 math worksheet builds confidence with addition within 100. Students use place-value understanding to add tens and ones, practise recording number sentences and check their work with the included answer key.

What’s Included

  • Two-digit addition problems within 100
  • Place-value scaffolds for tens and ones
  • Space to show working
  • Mixed practice sets for independent work
  • Student self-check prompts
  • Teacher answer key

Skills Practiced

  • Adding within 100
  • Using place value (tens and ones)
  • Recording addition number sentences
  • Applying regrouping when needed
  • Checking answers for reasonableness

Easy Ways to Use the Worksheet

Flexible practice for teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

In the Classroom

Model one problem with base-ten blocks or drawings, then release students to complete independent sets. Use the answer key for quick feedback groups.

At Home

Ask your child to explain how they added the tens and ones. Use coins or sticks to represent tens and ones when a problem feels tricky.

Duration, Sequencing and Calendar Time

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support use familiar time language to sequence events and discuss duration. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Use familiar time language to sequence events and discuss duration.

Teacher guidance

Connect time ideas to the daily routine. Use a calendar to discuss yesterday, today and tomorrow. Ask students to sequence familiar events and explain which take longer or less time.

Try it at home

Talk through the order of a familiar routine. Ask which step takes the longest and which takes the shortest time.

Support and extension

Use picture prompts and a three-step sequence. Extend by asking how a calendar helps plan an event later in the week.

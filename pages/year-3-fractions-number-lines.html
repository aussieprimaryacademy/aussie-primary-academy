Fractions on Number Lines

Help Year 3 students understand fractions by placing fractions correctly on number lines. This free printable Maths worksheet supports Australian Curriculum learning and includes practical classroom activities.

Learning Intention

Students will recognise, represent and place simple fractions on number lines.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M3N02

Recognise and represent unit fractions including 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5 and 1/10 and their multiples in different ways.

Teacher Notes

Use this worksheet after introducing fraction concepts. Encourage students to explain how they identified equal parts on the number line.

Skills Practised

  • Fraction recognition
  • Number line understanding
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Problem solving

Likes, Dislikes and Giving Reasons

Express a preference and give a simple reason to support an opinion. Use this printable in class, tutoring or at home with discussion and supported practice.

Year 1EnglishFree PDF

Learning focus

Express a preference and give a simple reason to support an opinion.

Teacher guidance

Model a clear opinion followed by because and a reason. Encourage students to listen to a different preference and respond respectfully rather than simply repeating their own view.

Try it at home

Ask your child to choose a favourite book, game or food and explain why. Model giving a reason using because.

Support and extension

Offer sentence starters such as I like... because... Extend by asking students to compare two choices and give a reason for each.

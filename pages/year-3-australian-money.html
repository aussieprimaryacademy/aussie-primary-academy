Learning Intention

Students will recognise Australian money and calculate totals and change in real-life contexts.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M3M06

Represent money values in various ways and calculate change from transactions involving one or more items.

Teacher Notes

Use real or play money for hands-on practice. Start with counting coins then move to total and change problems.

Skills Practised

  • Recognising Australian coins and notes
  • Calculating totals
  • Calculating change
  • Real-life money problems

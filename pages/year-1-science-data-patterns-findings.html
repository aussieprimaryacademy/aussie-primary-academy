Science Data, Patterns and Findings

Organise observations and use simple patterns to communicate findings. Use this resource with observation, discussion and safe hands-on learning.

Year 1ScienceFree PDF

Learning focus

Organise observations and use simple patterns to communicate findings.

Teacher guidance

Return to the question being investigated. Help students organise observations in a clear table or display, notice a pattern and state a finding that is supported by the recorded evidence.

Try it at home

After a simple observation, ask what was noticed more than once and what the information might show. Encourage your child to point to the evidence.

Support and extension

Use a small set of observations and a prepared table. Extend by asking students to explain what their evidence does and does not show.

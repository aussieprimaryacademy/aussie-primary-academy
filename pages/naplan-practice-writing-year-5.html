About this worksheet

This free printable NAPLAN-style Writing worksheet gives Year 5 students authentic practice with the writing task format. Students receive a stimulus and are guided to plan, write and review a complete narrative or persuasive text within a realistic time frame.

Ideal for classroom writing lessons, timed practice, or parent-supported home revision.

Learning Intention

Students will plan, create and edit a sustained written text (narrative or persuasive) that demonstrates clear structure, appropriate language features and careful attention to the NAPLAN writing criteria.

Skills Practised

  • Planning and organising ideas
  • Writing engaging openings and effective endings
  • Developing characters, setting or persuasive arguments
  • Using paragraphs and text connectives
  • Applying sophisticated vocabulary and sentence variety
  • Editing for spelling, punctuation and clarity

How to use this worksheet

  1. Introduce the prompt and discuss the purpose (narrative or persuasive).
  2. Allow 5–10 minutes for planning using the provided space.
  3. Students write for approximately 30 minutes.
  4. Reserve a few minutes at the end for editing and proofreading.
  5. Share strong examples and discuss against the NAPLAN marking criteria (audience, text structure, ideas, vocabulary, cohesion, paragraphing, sentence structure, punctuation, spelling).

Differentiation

Support: Provide sentence starters, a word bank, or a simplified planning scaffold. Allow extra time or a scribe if needed.

Extension: Challenge students to include specific literary devices or a counter-argument, or to write a second draft improving one criterion.

EAL/D: Pre-teach key vocabulary related to the prompt and allow oral rehearsal before writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the real NAPLAN writing task be narrative or persuasive?

ACARA announces the genre (narrative or persuasive) closer to the test window each year. Practising both is the best preparation.

How long should students write for?

The official NAPLAN Writing test allows 40 minutes total (including planning and editing). This worksheet is designed around that timing.

Can I use this for assessment?

Yes. It works well as a formative assessment piece or as practice before a summative writing task.

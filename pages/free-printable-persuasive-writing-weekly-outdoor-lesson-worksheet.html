About this worksheet

This Year 5 persuasive writing worksheet uses a weekly outdoor lesson as a school-based topic. It provides a context for forming a position and communicating reasons to an audience.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 5 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Building a persuasive case with a clear position and relevant reasons.

What students practise

Consider possible benefits and concerns about a weekly outdoor lesson. Choose a position and support it with reasons relevant to the topic.

How to use it

Print the PDF and identify the intended audience before writing. Afterwards, check whether the position is clear and the reasons are relevant.

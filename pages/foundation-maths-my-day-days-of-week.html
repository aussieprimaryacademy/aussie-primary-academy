About this worksheet

This Foundation Maths worksheet helps students learn the days of the week and practise sequencing familiar events in a day. Clear activities support early understanding of time and order.

Ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

Students will sequence the days of the week and order familiar events in a day.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9MFM02 — Sequence days of the week, months of the year and seasons, and connect familiar events to these time frames.

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent publisher and is not endorsed by ACARA.

Skills Practised

  • Days of the week
  • Sequencing events
  • Ordering daily routines
  • Time language
  • Calendar awareness

Teacher / Parent Notes

Link the worksheet to the class calendar and morning routine. Sing the days-of-the-week song before starting.

How to use this worksheet

  • Whole class: Explicit teaching + guided practice
  • Small groups: Sequencing activities with picture cards
  • Homework: Send home for extra practice
  • Tutoring / Homeschool: Clear layout makes it easy to use independently

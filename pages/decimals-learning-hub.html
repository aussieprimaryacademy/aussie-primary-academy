Decimals Learning Hub

Build decimal understanding step by step—from tenths and hundredths on a place-value chart, through comparing and calculating, to practical links with fractions, money and percentages.

Years 4–6 Australian Curriculum v9.0 Pathway in progress

What students practise

Decimal place value (tenths and hundredths); reading and writing decimals; comparing and ordering; adding and subtracting decimals; and connecting decimals with fractions and percentages in everyday contexts.

How to use this hub

Start with place value and concrete models (money, metric measures, number lines). Estimate before calculating. Link every decimal back to a fraction of a whole where possible.

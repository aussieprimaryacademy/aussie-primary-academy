What students will practise

  • Compare angles with a right angle
  • Use angle names to describe size
  • Estimate which of two angles is larger

Learning intention

Compare and estimate angles using angle names and relate them to a right angle.

How to use this worksheet

Ask the learner to identify a right angle first. Then compare each angle with that benchmark and explain whether it is smaller, equal to, or larger than a right angle.

Curriculum connection

AC9M4M04 — Estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Use the activities alongside your school’s teaching sequence and the learner’s current needs.

Compare and Estimate Angle Types — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents, teachers, tutors and homeschool families.

Is this Year 4 angle types worksheet free?

Yes. You can download the printable worksheet directly from this page without signing up.

What do children practise?

Children practise comparing and estimating angle sizes and using angle names such as acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution.

What if my child is unsure about angle names?

Start by comparing angles with the square corner of a page. A right angle is the reference point for the other angle names.

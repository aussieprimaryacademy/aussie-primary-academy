Science Questions and Predictions

Ask a testable question and make a prediction before an investigation. Use this resource with observation, discussion and safe hands-on learning.

Year 1ScienceFree PDF

Learning focus

Ask a testable question and make a prediction before an investigation.

Teacher guidance

Help students turn curiosity into a clear question about something they can observe. Ask for a prediction and a simple reason, then plan how to observe safely.

Try it at home

When your child asks why something happens, help turn the idea into a question that could be explored safely. Ask what they think might happen.

Support and extension

Offer a choice of investigation questions. Extend by asking students to identify what they would observe to help answer their question.

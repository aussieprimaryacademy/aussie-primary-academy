About this worksheet

This Year 1 English worksheet uses the idea of a surprise inside a school bag as a writing prompt. It can help children generate an idea, describe it and develop confidence expressing their thinking in writing.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 1 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Generating an imaginative idea and communicating it in writing.

What students practise

Talk briefly about what a surprising object might be and why it would be unexpected. Encourage the learner to use their own idea and follow the task instructions printed on the worksheet.

How to use it

Read the prompt together, invite the learner to explain their idea aloud, and then let them complete the printable activity. Review the writing by noticing one clear idea before choosing one next step.

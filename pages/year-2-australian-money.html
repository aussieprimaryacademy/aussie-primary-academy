Learning Intention

Students will recognise Australian coins and notes and calculate simple amounts of money.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M2M06

Recognise Australian coins and notes and solve simple problems involving money.

Teacher Notes

Use real or plastic coins where possible. Focus on identifying coins by size, colour and value before moving to simple addition of money amounts.

Skills Practised

  • Coin recognition
  • Note recognition
  • Counting money
  • Simple money problems

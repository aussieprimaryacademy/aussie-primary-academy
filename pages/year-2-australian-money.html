Learning Intention

Students will recognise Australian coins and notes and count small amounts of money.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M2M02

Identify and compare Australian coins and notes and use them in simple money situations.

Teacher Notes

Use real or plastic coins if possible. Practise counting collections and making the same amount in different ways.

Skills Practised

  • Recognising coins and notes
  • Counting money
  • Comparing amounts
  • Practical money sense

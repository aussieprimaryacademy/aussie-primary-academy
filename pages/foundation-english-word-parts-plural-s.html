About this worksheet

This Foundation English worksheet introduces the plural -s ending. Students practise turning singular words into plurals by adding -s.

Ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

Students will recognise and use the plural -s ending to show more than one.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9EFLY15 — Understand that a letter can represent more than one sound, and that a syllable must contain a vowel sound; explore word families and simple morphological units.

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use real objects (one pencil / two pencils) to make the idea concrete before writing.

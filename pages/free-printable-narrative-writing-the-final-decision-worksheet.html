About this worksheet

This Year 5 narrative writing worksheet uses a final decision as a story prompt. Students can explore a character's choice and how it affects what happens next.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 5 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Developing a narrative around a significant choice and its consequences.

What students practise

Consider who must make the decision, why it is difficult and what could happen afterwards. Use the worksheet's instructions to guide the writing.

How to use it

Print the PDF and discuss possible choices without choosing for the learner. Afterwards, check whether the decision connects to the story events.

About this worksheet

This Foundation Reading worksheet introduces early print concepts. Students learn about the front cover, back cover, title, author and how print is organised on a page.

Ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

Students will identify the parts of a book and demonstrate early understanding of how print works.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9EFLA04 — Understand conventions of print including left-to-right and top-to-bottom directionality, and the parts of a book.

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent publisher and is not endorsed by ACARA.

Skills Practised

  • Parts of a book
  • Print directionality
  • Book handling
  • Title and author awareness
  • Early literacy concepts

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use a real picture book alongside the worksheet. Point to the cover, title and pages while talking about how books work.

How to use this worksheet

  • Whole class: Explicit teaching + guided practice
  • Small groups: Book exploration activities
  • Homework: Send home for extra practice
  • Tutoring / Homeschool: Clear layout makes it easy to use independently

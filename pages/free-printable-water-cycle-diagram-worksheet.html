What is this water cycle diagram worksheet?

This free printable supports learners in reading a water-cycle diagram, recognising the process names and explaining what the arrows show. It is designed for introductory or extension use with Year 3 learners; the direct Australian Curriculum content connection is Year 4 Science AC9S4U02.

Learning intention

I can identify the main water-cycle processes on a diagram and explain how water moves through the environment.

Key vocabulary

Evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff.

Who can use it?

Teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families can use the page for independent practice or guided discussion.

What students practise

  • Reading the labels and arrows in a water-cycle diagram.
  • Matching process names to what happens to water.
  • Explaining the cycle in their own words.
  • Spotting vocabulary or process ideas that need revision.

How to use this PDF

  1. Review the words evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff.
  2. Download and print the diagram worksheet.
  3. Ask the learner to label or explain each process as directed on the worksheet.
  4. Ask them to explain one arrow without simply reading the label.

Curriculum note

AC9S4U02 is the Year 4 water-cycle content connection. This page does not describe the topic as required Year 3 curriculum content.

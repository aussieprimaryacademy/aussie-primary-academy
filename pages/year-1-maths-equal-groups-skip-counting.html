Equal Groups and Skip Counting

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support notice equal groups and count collections in equal steps. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Notice equal groups and count collections in equal steps.

Teacher guidance

Build groups with counters or classroom objects before recording a number sequence. Ask students to point to each group as they count; focus on understanding equal groups, not chanting alone.

Try it at home

Make small groups of two or five objects and count them together. Point to each group as you say the next number.

Support and extension

Start with groups of two and a small collection. Extend by representing the same total using different group sizes.

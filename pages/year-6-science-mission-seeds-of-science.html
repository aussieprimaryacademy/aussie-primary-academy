Year 6 Science Mission: Seeds of Science

Could you solve a real-world problem using science? Explore the scientific inquiry cycle, life cycles & survival, Earth, Sun & shadows, heat, energy & materials, plus STEM challenges.

Teacher Answers Available Separately

Learning Intention

Students will use the scientific inquiry cycle to ask questions, design fair tests, identify variables, collect evidence and solve real-world problems related to life cycles, shadows, heat transfer and materials.

Australian Curriculum

Aligned to Year 6 Science Understanding (Biological Sciences, Earth & Space Sciences, Physical Sciences) and Science Inquiry Skills. Supports questioning, predicting, planning investigations, processing and analysing data, and evaluating.

Teacher Notes

Perfect for National Science Week 2026. Use as a complete mission pack or individual pages. Safety note included for the insulation investigation (teacher handles hot water). Encourage students to complete the final “Your Science Mission” page with a real problem from their school or community.

Skills Practised

  • Scientific inquiry cycle
  • Identifying independent, dependent and controlled variables
  • Life cycles, adaptation and environmental conditions
  • How shadows change with the Sun’s position
  • Conductors vs insulators and heat transfer
  • Designing and evaluating fair tests
  • Higher-order reasoning and evidence-based conclusions
  • Applying science to real-world STEM challenges

What students will practise

  • Recognise angles that measure 90 degrees
  • Find right angles in shapes and everyday objects
  • Use a right angle as a comparison tool

Learning intention

Identify right angles and use them as a benchmark for comparing other angles.

How to use this worksheet

Show the learner the corner of a sheet of paper or a set square. Compare each angle with that corner and look for right angles in the room.

Curriculum connection

AC9M4M04 — Estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Use the activities alongside your school’s teaching sequence and the learner’s current needs.

Recognising Right Angles — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents, teachers, tutors and homeschool families.

How big is a right angle?

A right angle measures exactly 90 degrees.

Is this a free printable worksheet?

Yes. You can download the worksheet PDF directly without signing up.

Where can children find right angles in real life?

Common examples include the corners of books, tiles, windows and many rectangular objects.

What comes after recognising right angles?

Practise comparing acute and obtuse angles, then use the Angles Learning Hub to build the next step.

Area of a Rectangle

Calculate rectangle areas, find missing dimensions and apply area skills to practical problems.

Maths AC9M6M02 Australian Curriculum-aligned

Worksheet Preview

⬇ Download Free PDF

Learning Intention

Students calculate the area of rectangles and solve practical area problems.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Year 6 Maths learning. (AC9M6M02)

Teacher Notes

Connect area calculations to real-world contexts like flooring or garden beds.

Skills Practised

  • Area calculation
  • Solving for missing dimensions
  • Practical measurement

What Students Will Practise

  • Calculating the area of rectangles
  • Finding missing side lengths when area is known
  • Applying area formulas to practical problems
  • Checking whether answers are reasonable

How to Use This Worksheet

  • Classwork: Guided practice after introducing the area formula.
  • Homework: Independent application of area skills.
  • Revision: Quick review before a measurement assessment.
  • Small-group support: Model multi-step area problems together.

About this worksheet

This Year 6 narrative writing worksheet focuses on two viewpoints. Students can consider how perspective shapes what a character notices, thinks or communicates about a situation.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 6 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Exploring perspective and developing a narrative that considers two viewpoints.

What students practise

Discuss how two characters might interpret the same event differently. Encourage the learner to make each viewpoint understandable and follow the worksheet's directions.

How to use it

Print the PDF and consider what each character knows or believes before writing. Afterwards, check whether the viewpoints are distinguishable and make sense in the story.

About this worksheet

This Year 4 persuasive writing worksheet asks students to consider a quiet reading space. The topic offers a familiar school-based context for expressing a position and giving reasons to influence an audience.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 4 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Stating a position and supporting it with relevant reasons.

What students practise

Ask the learner what a quiet reading space could offer students and what someone might disagree with. Encourage reasons that support their own position, following the worksheet's directions.

How to use it

Print the PDF and discuss the audience before writing. Afterwards, check whether the position is clear and whether each reason helps persuade the intended reader.

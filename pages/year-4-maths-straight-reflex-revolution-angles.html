What students will practise

  • Recognise a straight angle as 180 degrees
  • Identify reflex angles greater than 180 degrees and less than 360 degrees
  • Connect a revolution with one full turn

Learning intention

Recognise straight, reflex and revolution angles and connect them with half, more-than-half and full turns.

How to use this worksheet

Use a clock hand or a simple turning movement to discuss half turns and full turns. Compare the angle opening with a straight line and a complete turn.

Curriculum connection

AC9M4M04 — Estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Straight, Reflex and Revolution Angles — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents, teachers, tutors and homeschool families.

What is a straight angle?

A straight angle measures 180 degrees, which is half a full turn.

What is a reflex angle?

A reflex angle is greater than 180 degrees and smaller than 360 degrees.

What is a revolution angle?

A revolution represents one complete turn of 360 degrees.

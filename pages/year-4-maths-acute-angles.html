What students will practise

  • Recognise angles smaller than 90 degrees
  • Compare acute angles with a right angle
  • Explain how the angle opening shows its size

Learning intention

Identify and describe acute angles by comparing them with a right angle.

How to use this worksheet

Use the corner of a piece of paper as a right-angle checker. Ask the learner to compare each angle with the corner and explain why an acute angle is smaller.

Curriculum connection

AC9M4M04 — Estimate and compare angles using angle names including acute, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution, and recognise their relationship to a right angle.

Use the activities alongside your school’s teaching sequence and the learner’s current needs.

Recognising Acute Angles — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents, teachers, tutors and homeschool families.

What is an acute angle?

An acute angle is smaller than a right angle, so it measures less than 90 degrees.

Is this worksheet free to print?

Yes. The worksheet is available as a free printable download without a sign-up.

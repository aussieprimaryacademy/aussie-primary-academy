Science Brain Break Quiz

A no-writing, movement-based science quiz perfect for National Science Week. Teachers read questions aloud; students stand, sit or spin/jump to show their answer. Includes Lower Primary (F–2) and Upper Primary (Y3–6) quizzes plus a full answer key with fun Did You Know facts.

Includes Lower Primary + Upper Primary quizzes and Answer Key with Did You Know facts

How to play

The teacher (or parent) reads each question aloud. Students choose an answer, then do the matching action — no writing needed. It is a quiz and a movement break in one.

  • A = Stand Up
  • B = Sit Down
  • C = Spin / Jump

What is included

  • Lower Primary Quiz (Foundation–Year 2) — 10 questions covering biological, earth & space and chemical sciences
  • Upper Primary Quiz (Year 3–6) — 10 questions covering photosynthesis, solar system, organs, gravity, marsupials and more
  • Answer Key with correct answers and short Did You Know science facts

Australian Curriculum

Supports Science Understanding and Science Inquiry Skills across Foundation to Year 6. Ideal as a quick review, transition activity or National Science Week warm-up.

Teacher Notes

Works well as a whole-class brain break, small-group warm-up or home activity. No materials needed beyond the printed quiz. Answers can be revealed one by one or at the end using the answer key page.

Skills Practised

  • Science knowledge recall (biological, chemical, earth & space, physical sciences)
  • Listening and following multi-step instructions
  • Active participation and movement
  • Quick thinking under low pressure

Number Lines and Number Charts to 120

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support use number lines and charts to locate, order and compare numbers. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Use number lines and charts to locate, order and compare numbers.

Teacher guidance

Model how to locate a number and move one place forward or back. Ask students to notice patterns across rows and columns and explain how the chart helps.

Try it at home

Use a homemade number line to locate numbers. Ask which numbers come before and after a chosen number.

Support and extension

Start with a short section of the chart. Extend by asking students to use a pattern to locate a number without counting every step.

About this worksheet

This Foundation Maths worksheet introduces young learners to addition through simple, everyday stories. Students practise counting, combining groups and solving basic word problems in a clear, accessible format.

Ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

Students will solve simple addition word problems by counting and combining groups of objects.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9MFN05 — Represent practical situations involving addition using physical and virtual materials and number sentences.

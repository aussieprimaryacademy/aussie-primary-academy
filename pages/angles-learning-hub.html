Angles Learning Hub

Build angle understanding step by step—from recognising and comparing angle types in Year 4, to estimating and measuring degrees in Year 5, then solving missing-angle and angle-relationship problems in Year 6.

Years 4–6Free printable resourcesAustralian Curriculum

What students practise

Recognising acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex and revolution angles; estimating and measuring degrees; using a protractor; and reasoning about missing angles and angle relationships.

How to use this hub

Start with the year level that matches the student’s current learning. Estimate before measuring, explain the reasoning aloud, then use the next worksheet to build confidence. Curriculum codes are shown as listed on the related resource pages and should be checked against your school’s sequence.

Parent and teacher tip

Ask students to estimate first, name the angle relationship, and explain why their answer makes sense. Encourage a labelled diagram and one sentence of reasoning—not just a number.

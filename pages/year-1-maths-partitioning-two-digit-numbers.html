Partitioning Two-Digit Numbers

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support represent two-digit numbers as groups of tens and leftover ones. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Represent two-digit numbers as groups of tens and leftover ones.

Teacher guidance

Use bundles of ten or base-ten materials. Ask students to build a number, identify the tens and ones, and explain the value of each digit.

Try it at home

Choose a two-digit number from a calendar or house number. Ask how many tens and ones it contains.

Support and extension

Use concrete bundles of ten first. Extend by showing the same number in more than one partition and explaining why the total stays the same.

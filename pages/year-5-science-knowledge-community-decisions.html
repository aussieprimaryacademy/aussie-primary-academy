About This Worksheet

This worksheet helps Year 5 students investigate how scientific knowledge is used to inform decisions in communities. Students practise linking evidence to choices that affect people and places.

Aligned to AC9S5H02 — investigate how scientific knowledge is used to inform decisions in communities.

Skills Practised

Saving Dripping Water: Reading Comprehension

Understand an informational text and connect its ideas with everyday actions. Use the printable alongside supported reading and conversation.

Year 1ReadingFree PDF

Learning focus

Understand an informational text and connect its ideas with everyday actions.

Teacher guidance

Preview key vocabulary and discuss the topic before reading. Afterwards, ask students to identify the main message and distinguish information stated in the text from their own ideas.

Try it at home

Notice a way your family saves water. Read or discuss a short information text and ask which details explain why the action matters.

Support and extension

Use pictures or key words to support understanding. Extend by asking students to summarise the main message in their own words and identify a supporting detail.

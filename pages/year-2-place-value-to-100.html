Learning Intention

Students will recognise tens and ones, represent numbers to 100 and compare two-digit numbers.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M2N01

Recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 100 using place value understanding.

Teacher Notes

Use MAB blocks or ten-frames first. Then move to expanded form and comparing numbers with greater than / less than.

Skills Practised

  • Tens and ones
  • Representing numbers to 100
  • Comparing two-digit numbers
  • Place value language

About this worksheet

This three-page Foundation English worksheet gives students practice with capital letters and sentence-ending punctuation. Students edit four short sentences, write three sentences about a park picture idea and use a checklist to review their writing. The final page includes suggested answers and a teacher/parent guide.

Learning intention

We are learning to use capital letters and end punctuation in short sentences.

Success looks like: starting sentences with a capital letter, using capitals for names, choosing an appropriate end mark and checking written sentences.

Curriculum alignment

AC9EFLA09: Identify punctuation as a feature of written text and recognise the use of capital letters and sentence-ending punctuation.

AC9EFLY06: Create and participate in shared editing of short written texts using learnt vocabulary and basic sentence punctuation.

This worksheet provides focused practice related to these Australian Curriculum v9.0 descriptions; it does not represent full coverage of either description.

Skills practised

  • Editing sentence beginnings and names with capital letters.
  • Choosing a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark to suit a sentence.
  • Rewriting short sentences accurately.
  • Writing simple sentences from a picture idea.
  • Checking work against a short editing checklist.

Teacher and parent notes

Ask the student to read each sentence aloud before choosing its end punctuation. The answer guide gives suggested corrections and notes that a full stop may be accepted where the tone permits. Use the final-page formative check to note whether the student works independently, with a prompt or needs the skill revisited.

How to use this worksheet

  1. Work through the four editing sentences on page 1.
  2. Use the park picture idea on page 2 to write three sentences, then complete the self-check.
  3. Use page 3 to review suggested answers and identify a next step.

Differentiation

Support: Model one sentence first. Read each sentence aloud together and prompt the student to check the first letter and final punctuation mark.

Core: Complete the editing tasks and write three sentences using the checklist.

Extend: Add detail to the park sentences and explain why each sentence needs its chosen end mark.

Frequently asked questions

Are answers included?
Yes. Page 3 includes suggested answers and a teacher/parent guide.

Does the worksheet include original writing?
Yes. Page 2 asks students to write three sentences about a day at the park.

Can it be used at home?
Yes. An adult can read the instructions and discuss the student's punctuation choices.

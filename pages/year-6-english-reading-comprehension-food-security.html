About this worksheet

This Year 6 reading comprehension activity helps students practise reading and responding to an informational text about food security — reliable access to enough safe and nutritious food for a growing world population.

Students locate information, use the built-in vocabulary helper, and answer literal and inferential questions. The passage also introduces useful real-world ideas: climate impacts on crops, pressures on Australian agriculture (drought, flood and biosecurity), emerging food technologies, and questions about fairness in global food systems.

That extra knowledge is a natural bonus of a well-chosen reading text — not a separate Science lesson. The main skill practised here is Reading.

Instructions are clear, the text is age-appropriate for Year 6, and a full answer key is included in the same PDF.

Learning Intention

Students will read and comprehend an informational text and demonstrate understanding by answering literal and inferential questions.

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use as a warm-up, guided reading task or independent homework. The vocabulary helper (destabilised, biosecurity, drought-resistant, inequitable, political will) works well as a short discussion before reading.

Answers are included at the end of the PDF. Suitable for mainstream Year 6, tutoring and homeschool programs across Australia.

🌱 Bonus knowledge from the reading

While practising reading skills, students encounter real-world ideas from the passage, including:

  • Global population growth and the challenge of feeding more people
  • Climate change and its impact on crop yields
  • Australian agriculture — drought, flood and biosecurity pressures
  • Emerging approaches such as drought-resistant crops, vertical farming, lab-grown meat and precision fermentation
  • Fairness in food systems — distribution, pricing, hunger and inequality

This is background knowledge from the text, not a formal Science assessment.

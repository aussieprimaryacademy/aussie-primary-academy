About this worksheet

This Year 6 reading comprehension activity helps students practise reading and responding to an informational text. The passage introduces the idea of food security — having reliable access to enough safe and nutritious food — and asks students to locate information, build vocabulary and make inferences.

Parents and teachers can use this activity to practise an important Reading skill while naturally introducing Science-related vocabulary and ideas about food systems and living things.

Instructions are clear, the text is age-appropriate for Year 6, and a full answer key is included in the same PDF.

Learning Intention

Students will read and comprehend an informational text and demonstrate understanding by answering literal and inferential questions.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9E6LY04 — Analyse how text structures and language features work together to meet the purpose of a text and engage audiences.

Primary alignment is Year 6 English (Reading). Aussie Primary Academy is an independent publisher and is not endorsed by ACARA.

Skills Practised

  • Reading comprehension
  • Locating key information
  • Inferring meaning from context
  • Understanding informational text features

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use as a warm-up, guided reading task or independent homework. Discuss unfamiliar vocabulary before reading if needed. Answers are included at the end of the PDF.

Suitable for mainstream Year 6, tutoring and homeschool programs across Australia.

About this worksheet

This Year 6 Science worksheet helps students understand the relationship between living things and the physical conditions of their habitats. Students explore temperature, light, water and other factors that influence survival.

Learning Intention

Students will describe how physical conditions in a habitat affect the survival of living things.

Curriculum Alignment

Supports Year 6 Biological Sciences (AC9S6U01) and Science Inquiry Skills.

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent publisher and is not endorsed by ACARA.

Skills Practised

  • Identifying physical conditions
  • Linking conditions to survival
  • Comparing different habitats
  • Scientific observation and reasoning

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use as an introduction to habitats or as a follow-up after outdoor observations.

How to use this worksheet

Extend: Research a local habitat and present how physical conditions affect two living things.

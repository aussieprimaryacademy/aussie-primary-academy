About This Year 2 Character & Setting Worksheet

This Year 2 English resource moves students beyond simply naming a character or setting. Students look closely at the words and phrases an author uses to describe people, places and story details.

Students then connect those language clues to what they understand and imagine about the story. This supports reading comprehension, vocabulary development and early evidence-based responses.

It is part of the My Story Symphony – Book Week 2026 collection from Aussie Primary Academy.

Learning Intention

“I can find words that help me understand a character and setting.”

Australian Curriculum V9

AC9E2LE03

Teacher / Parent Tip

Model the difference between simply naming a character or place and finding the exact words that create the description.

Ask students to highlight or underline the evidence first, then explain what that detail helps them understand or imagine.

Differentiation

Support: Underline one describing word together first, then ask students to find another word or phrase independently.

Core: Students locate descriptive language and explain what it tells them about the character or setting.

Extend: Ask students to replace one descriptive phrase with their own and explain how the new wording changes the picture in the reader's mind.

Why This Helps Readers

Strong readers notice that authors choose words for a reason. Identifying precise character and setting language helps students visualise a story, build vocabulary and begin explaining how authors create meaning.

How to Use This Worksheet

  • Whole class: Model how to find a descriptive word and explain what it reveals.
  • Guided reading: Highlight character and setting clues together before students answer independently.
  • Independent practice: Students locate evidence and explain what the words help them understand or imagine.
  • Home learning: Parents can ask, “Which words helped you picture the character or place?”

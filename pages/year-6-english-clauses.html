Learning Intention

Students identify and use main, subordinate and relative clauses to build complex sentences.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Year 6 English learning. (AC9E6LA06)

Teacher Notes

Colour-code clauses in sample sentences to make the structure visible.

Skills Practised

  • Clause identification
  • Complex sentence construction
  • Grammar analysis

What Students Will Practise

  • Identifying main, subordinate and relative clauses
  • Combining ideas into controlled complex sentences
  • Understanding how clauses change meaning and emphasis
  • Improving sentence variety in writing

How to Use This Worksheet

  • Classwork: Guided identification and sentence combining.
  • Homework: Independent clause practice.
  • Revision: Grammar review before writing tasks.
  • Small-group support: Colour-code clauses together to make structure clear.

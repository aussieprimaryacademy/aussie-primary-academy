About This Holiday Pack

The Year 6 Spring School Holiday Learning Pack is a low-pressure 5-day routine. Each day is short (15–20 minutes), calm and encouraging — not a holiday test.

Families complete one session a day. Grown-ups read directions together, let the child try, and stop after about 20 minutes. The goal is confidence and continuity into Term 4.

5-Day Plan

  • Day 1 — Read and respond
  • Day 2 — Word work and writing
  • Day 3 — Number and operations
  • Day 4 — Applied maths
  • Day 5 — Challenge and reflect

What's Included

  • Parent guide and calm holiday routine
  • Cover page with name and start date
  • Critical literacy reading activity
  • Word work and writing practice
  • Number and operations tasks
  • Applied mathematics activity
  • Screen-free challenge and reflection
  • Answer key and adult guidance

Skills Practised

  • Critical literacy and evidence-based response
  • Precise argument and writing craft
  • Number and operations
  • Applied mathematics
  • Challenge and reflection

Teacher & Parent Notes

Use the parent script: “Show me what you notice. Which part can you try? If you get stuck, we will solve one example together.”

Mistakes are information, not failure. The purpose is confidence and continuity, not holiday testing.

How to Use the Pack

  1. Print the PDF (A4 works well).
  2. Write the child’s name and start date on the cover.
  3. Complete one short session each day for five days.
  4. Read directions together; let your child try independently where possible.
  5. Use the answer key for quick checking.
  6. On Day 5, celebrate one success and note one Term 4 goal.

About this worksheet

This Foundation Maths worksheet introduces the idea of equal sharing and making equal groups. Students practise sharing objects fairly and recognising when groups are equal.

Ready to print and use for whole-class lessons, small groups, homework or home learning.

Learning Intention

Students will share collections of objects equally and make equal groups.

Curriculum Alignment

AC9MFN06 — Represent practical situations that involve equal sharing and grouping with physical materials and use counting strategies to find solutions.

Aussie Primary Academy is an independent publisher and is not endorsed by ACARA.

Skills Practised

  • Equal sharing
  • Making equal groups
  • Counting strategies
  • Fair sharing language
  • Early division concepts

Teacher / Parent Notes

Use real objects (counters, blocks, fruit) to act out sharing before independent work. Talk about “fair shares”.

How to use this worksheet

  • Whole class: Explicit teaching + guided practice
  • Small groups: Hands-on sharing activities
  • Homework: Send home for extra practice
  • Tutoring / Homeschool: Clear layout makes it easy to use independently

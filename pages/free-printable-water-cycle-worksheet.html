What is this water cycle worksheet about?

This free printable helps primary learners practise the main water-cycle processes and explain how water moves between the land, water bodies and air. It is intended as introductory or extension practice for Year 3; the direct Australian Curriculum content connection is Year 4 Science AC9S4U02.

Learning intention

I can name key water-cycle processes and explain, in simple words, how water changes or moves through the environment.

Key vocabulary

Evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff.

Who can use it?

Australian primary teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families. Adults can read the questions aloud or discuss a real-life example before independent practice.

What will students practise?

  • Recognising the main stages of the water cycle.
  • Connecting scientific words to everyday examples.
  • Explaining a process rather than only naming it.
  • Identifying ideas that need more practice.

How to use this PDF

  1. Discuss a familiar example, such as a drying puddle or wet washing.
  2. Download and print the worksheet.
  3. Let the learner attempt each task and explain their thinking.
  4. Review answers using the resources supplied with the worksheet, if included.

Curriculum note

AC9S4U02 is the Year 4 water-cycle content connection. This page does not present water-cycle study as a required Year 3 content description.

Purpose of this resource

A transition conversation starter to help students recognise strengths, identify questions and supports, and plan for new routines.

What’s included

  • Reflect on strengths
  • prepare questions for the receiving school
  • practise organisation and timetable thinking
  • identify trusted adults
  • plan helpful first-week routines.

How teachers can use it

Use in short sessions during the final weeks of primary school. Invite independent, paired or supported work. Check school-specific details with the receiving school because routines and timetables differ.

Practical classroom approach

  • Explain the purpose and model an example.
  • Allow students to work at a comfortable pace.
  • Use responses to guide conversation, not as high-stakes assessment.

Guidance for parents and carers

Talk through the move at the child’s pace. Listen to questions and help identify a trusted adult at the new school. Confirm transport, uniform and timetable details with the receiving school.

Keep it supportive

Focus on listening, encouragement and the child’s own words. There is no need to complete every prompt in one sitting.

Inclusive ways to adapt the activities

Break tasks into short steps. Offer scribing, verbal or drawing responses and extra processing time. Students may leave questions blank.

Helpful adjustments

  • Offer short sessions and one-step instructions.
  • Accept drawing, speaking, pointing or adult scribing where appropriate.
  • Provide extra processing time and breaks.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for all Year 6 students?

It is designed for Year 6 students preparing for secondary school. Adapt it to each student’s pathway and receiving-school information.

Does it explain every secondary school’s routines?

No. It helps students prepare questions and practise planning. School routines and support arrangements vary.

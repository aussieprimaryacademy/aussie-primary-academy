Learning Intention

Students identify different animal habitats and explain how habitats meet animals' needs.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Year 1 Science learning about living things and how they depend on their environment.

Teacher Notes

Discuss examples of habitats such as forests, oceans, deserts and gardens. Encourage students to match animals with suitable homes.

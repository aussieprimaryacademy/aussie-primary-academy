About This Subtraction Within 10 Worksheet

Children begin with a whole group, remove part of it and count what remains. The gradual move from objects to pictures and equations builds a meaningful foundation for subtraction.

What Is Included

  • Picture subtraction within 10
  • Draw-and-cross-out practice
  • Take-away story problems
  • Mixed subtraction equations
  • Student reflection
  • Teacher answer key

Skills Practiced

  • Modeling subtraction as taking away
  • Counting how many remain
  • Connecting pictures and equations
  • Solving stories within 10
  • One-to-one counting
  • Explaining a strategy

In the Classroom

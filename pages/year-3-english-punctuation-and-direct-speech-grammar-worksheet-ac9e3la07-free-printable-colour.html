Learning Intention

Students will correctly punctuate direct speech using quotation marks, commas and capital letters.

Australian Curriculum

AC9E3LA07

Understand how dialogue is used in narratives and how to punctuate it correctly.

Teacher Notes

Model spoken sentences and show how they look in writing with speech marks. Practise with short conversations.

Skills Practised

  • Direct speech

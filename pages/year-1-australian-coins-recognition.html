Australian Coins Recognition

Help Year 1 students recognise Australian coins and match coin values through simple, practical money activities.

Learning Intention

Students will recognise Australian coins (5c, 10c, 20c, 50c, $1, $2) and match them to their values.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M1M03

Identify and describe the features of Australian coins and use them in simple money situations.

Teacher Notes

Use real or plastic coins if possible. Focus on recognising each coin by size, colour and value.

Skills Practised

  • Coin recognition
  • Coin values
  • Matching
  • Early financial literacy

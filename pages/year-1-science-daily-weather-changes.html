Daily Weather Changes

Observe and describe how weather can change from day to day. Use this resource with observation, discussion and safe hands-on learning.

Year 1ScienceFree PDF

Learning focus

Observe and describe how weather can change from day to day.

Teacher guidance

Build a short weather record using consistent observations. Model descriptive words for cloud, wind, rain and temperature, and distinguish what students observe from what they predict.

Try it at home

Look outside together and describe the weather using specific words. Compare it with yesterday without assuming the same pattern will continue.

Support and extension

Offer a small set of weather symbols and record one observation each day. Extend by asking students to describe a pattern across several days.

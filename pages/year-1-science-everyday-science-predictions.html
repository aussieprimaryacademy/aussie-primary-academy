Everyday Science Predictions

Make a prediction about a familiar event and explain the thinking behind it. Use this resource with observation, discussion and safe hands-on learning.

Year 1ScienceFree PDF

Learning focus

Make a prediction about a familiar event and explain the thinking behind it.

Teacher guidance

Ask students what they think will happen and why before observing. Record the prediction separately from the result so students can compare the two fairly.

Try it at home

Before a safe everyday test, ask your child what they think will happen and what makes them think so. Afterwards, compare the result with the prediction.

Support and extension

Offer two possible outcomes to choose from. Extend by asking students to explain what evidence would help test their prediction.

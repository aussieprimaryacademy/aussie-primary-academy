Learning Intention

Students will identify place value and represent numbers up to 10,000.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M3N01

Recognise, represent and order natural numbers up to 10,000 including place value understanding.

Teacher Notes

Start with concrete materials like MAB blocks. Then move to the worksheet. Ask students to explain their thinking.

Skills Practised

  • Identifying thousands, hundreds, tens and ones
  • Representing numbers to 10,000
  • Expanding numbers
  • Comparing and ordering numbers

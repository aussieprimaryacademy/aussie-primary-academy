About This Worksheet

This Foundation handwriting worksheet gives children guided practice forming lowercase letters. Repeated, careful practice can help children develop consistent letter shapes, direction and starting points before they move towards more fluent independent writing.

I Can Checklist

I can form lowercase letters carefully. I can start letters in the correct place. I can keep my letters a consistent size and direction.

How to Use It

Model one letter at a time before children begin. Demonstrate the starting point and movement, then let the child trace before trying the letter independently. Encourage slow, controlled movements first. Once the shape is familiar, practise writing the same letter without the guide.

Parent Tip

Keep handwriting practice short and positive. Practise a small group of letters rather than rushing through the whole alphabet. If a letter is difficult, model the movement again and let your child try it several times without pressure.

Learning Intention

Students practise correct letter formation for lowercase letters.

Australian Curriculum

Supports Foundation English learning. (AC9EFLY08)

Teacher Notes

Model correct pencil grip and starting points for each letter before students begin.

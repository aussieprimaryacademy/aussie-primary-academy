Fractions Learning Hub

Build fraction understanding step by step—from sharing into halves and quarters, through unit fractions and equivalence, to fraction–decimal connections in the upper primary years.

Years 1–6 Australian Curriculum v9.0 Pathway in progress

What students practise

Halves, quarters and eighths; unit fractions; fractions as parts of a whole and of a collection; equivalence; comparing and ordering; and linking fractions with decimals and percentages where appropriate.

How to use this hub

Start at the year level that matches the learner. Use concrete materials or drawings first, then move to number lines and symbols. Return to an earlier stage if a gap appears.

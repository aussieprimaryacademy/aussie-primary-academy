About this worksheet

This worksheet covers common Year 5 spelling patterns (including frequently misspelled words), grammar choices (such as subject–verb agreement with “neither…nor”), and punctuation decisions including colon use.

Ideal for literacy rotations, tutoring warm-ups or short homework tasks.

This is independent practice material inspired by NAPLAN-style question types. It is not an official NAPLAN test and is not endorsed by ACARA or education authorities.

Learning Intention

Students will practise Year 5 spelling, grammar and punctuation items in a NAPLAN-style format.

Curriculum / purpose

Supports Year 5 english skills through NAPLAN-style practice items. Useful general preparation for literacy assessment formats without claiming official test status.

Skills Practised

  • Spelling patterns and commonly confused words
  • Grammar accuracy
  • Punctuation for meaning
  • Multiple-choice test strategies

Teacher / Parent Notes

Encourage students to eliminate wrong options and explain why the correct spelling/grammar works. Pair with a short spelling list from errors.

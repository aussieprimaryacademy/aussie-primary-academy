About this worksheet

This Foundation English worksheet uses a playground picture as a prompt for early writing. It gives adults a practical starting point for talking about what a child can see and helping them turn an idea into written language.

Who is it for?

Designed for Foundation learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Picture-based writing and expressing an idea in words.

What students practise

Invite the learner to look closely at the playground picture, talk about what is happening, and complete the writing task shown on the worksheet. Model oral language first without writing the answer for the child.

How to use it

Print the PDF and look at the picture together. Ask the learner to describe one thing they notice, allow thinking time, and then let them complete the task independently or with age-appropriate support.

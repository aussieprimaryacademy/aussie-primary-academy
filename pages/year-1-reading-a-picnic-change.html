A Picnic Change: Reading Comprehension

Read a short text for meaning and explain how events or details connect. Use the printable alongside supported reading and conversation.

Year 1ReadingFree PDF

Learning focus

Read a short text for meaning and explain how events or details connect.

Teacher guidance

Before reading, discuss the title and invite a prediction. Afterwards, ask students to retell the key events in order and point to details that support their answers. Encourage complete spoken responses before writing.

Try it at home

Read a short story together and ask what changed, why it changed and which part of the text helped your child decide.

Support and extension

Read a small section at a time and use who, what, where and why prompts. Extend by asking students to explain a character's choice using evidence from the text.

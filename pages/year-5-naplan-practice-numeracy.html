About this worksheet

Students work through multiple-choice numeracy items covering place value, decimals, fractions, percentages, money, multi-step problems, perimeter, and statistics/problem solving using a data table.

Useful for mixed revision before assessment periods or as a diagnostic warm-up.

This is independent practice material inspired by NAPLAN-style question types. It is not an official NAPLAN test and is not endorsed by ACARA or education authorities.

Learning Intention

Students will practise Year 5 numeracy problems across number, measurement, geometry and statistics.

Curriculum / purpose

Supports Year 5 maths skills through NAPLAN-style practice items. Useful general preparation for numeracy assessment formats without claiming official test status.

Skills Practised

  • Place value and decimals
  • Fractions and percentages
  • Multi-step number problems
  • Perimeter and geometry thinking
  • Data interpretation

Teacher / Parent Notes

Encourage underlining key information and checking whether answers make sense. Discuss strategies for multi-step items.

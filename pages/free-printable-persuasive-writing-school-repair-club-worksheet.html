About this worksheet

This Year 6 persuasive writing worksheet uses a school repair club as a proposal to consider. Students can develop a position and explain reasons that may influence a school-based audience.

Who is it for?

Designed for Year 6 learners and can be used by classroom teachers, parents, tutors and homeschool families.

Skill focus

Presenting a position and supporting a proposal with relevant reasons.

What students practise

Consider what a repair club might do and why a school might or might not support the idea. Encourage relevant reasons and follow the worksheet instructions.

How to use it

Print the PDF and identify the audience or decision-maker the writing is intended to influence. Afterwards, check whether the position and reasons clearly support the proposal.

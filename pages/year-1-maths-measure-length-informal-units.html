Measuring Length with Informal Units

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support measure and compare length using repeated informal units. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Measure and compare length using repeated informal units.

Teacher guidance

Model measuring with equal-sized units placed end to end without gaps or overlaps. Discuss why using different-sized units changes the count.

Try it at home

Measure a book or table using identical blocks or paper clips. Discuss why the same unit matters when comparing results.

Support and extension

Provide identical units and a short object. Extend by measuring one object with two different units and explaining the different counts.

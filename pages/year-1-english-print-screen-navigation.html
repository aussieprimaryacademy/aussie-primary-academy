Navigating Print and Screen Texts

Recognise how readers navigate information in printed and digital texts. Use this printable in class, tutoring or at home with discussion and supported practice.

Year 1EnglishFree PDF

Learning focus

Recognise how readers navigate information in printed and digital texts.

Teacher guidance

Compare a book page with a child-friendly screen or website. Point out headings, page numbers, menus, icons and other features that help readers find information. Keep screen use supervised and purposeful.

Try it at home

When reading a book or a safe child-friendly page together, ask your child how they know where to look for a heading, page number or next section.

Support and extension

Use a familiar book and point to one feature at a time. Extend by asking students to explain which feature would help them find a particular piece of information.

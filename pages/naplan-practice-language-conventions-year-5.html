About this worksheet

This free printable NAPLAN-style Language Conventions worksheet gives Year 5 students realistic practice in the three key areas assessed: spelling, grammar and punctuation. Questions mirror the style and difficulty of the national test so students become familiar with the format before the real assessment.

Perfect for whole-class warm-ups, small-group intervention, homework, or independent revision in the weeks leading up to NAPLAN.

Learning Intention

Students will strengthen their ability to spell complex words accurately, apply grammatical knowledge in context, and use punctuation correctly so they can approach the NAPLAN Language Conventions test with confidence.

Australian Curriculum V9 Alignment

  • AC9E5LA05 – Understand that the structure of a complex sentence includes a main clause and at least one dependent clause
  • AC9E5LA06 – Understand how noun groups can be expanded
  • AC9E5LA08 – Understand how vocabulary is used to express greater precision of meaning
  • AC9E5LY06 – Plan, create, edit and publish written texts using appropriate punctuation including dialogue punctuation
  • Supports the Literacy general capability (spelling, grammar and punctuation progressions)

Skills Practised

  • Spelling of multisyllabic and less common words
  • Subject–verb agreement and tense consistency
  • Correct use of commas, apostrophes, quotation marks and capital letters
  • Identifying and correcting common grammatical errors
  • Understanding sentence structure (simple, compound and complex)
  • Vocabulary precision and word choice

How to use this worksheet

  1. Print one copy per student (or project on screen for shared practice).
  2. Allow 40–45 minutes under timed or untimed conditions depending on purpose.
  3. Review answers together, discussing the reasoning behind each correct response.
  4. Revisit any weak areas with targeted mini-lessons or additional practice.
  5. Use a second attempt later in the term to track growth.

Differentiation

Support: Read questions aloud, allow extra time, provide a spelling reference list, or complete selected questions only.

Extension: Ask students to write their own sentences demonstrating the grammar or punctuation rule, or create a short quiz for a partner.

EAL/D: Pre-teach key vocabulary and allow use of a bilingual dictionary where appropriate.

Part-Part-Whole: Splitting Numbers to 10

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support understand how a whole number can be made from two parts. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Understand how a whole number can be made from two parts.

Teacher guidance

Use counters or a ten frame to show a whole and its parts. This resource can support prerequisite review before Year 1 addition and subtraction.

Try it at home

Choose a number up to ten and show it as two groups of objects. Find another way to make the same total.

Support and extension

Use a ten frame and move counters between parts. Extend by recording several partitions of the same whole.

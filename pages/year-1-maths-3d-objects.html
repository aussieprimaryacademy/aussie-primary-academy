3D Objects: Features and Classification

A focused Year 1 Maths printable to support recognise and describe familiar three-dimensional objects. Use it in class, tutoring or at home alongside practical discussion and hands-on examples.

Year 1MathematicsFree PDF

Learning focus

Recognise and describe familiar three-dimensional objects.

Teacher guidance

Use a ball, box, can and cone. Let students handle each object, notice flat and curved surfaces, and explain which objects roll, stack or slide. Ask them to justify each sorting choice.

Try it at home

Find three household objects and compare how they move. Ask: Which rolls? Which stacks? What feature makes you think so?

Support and extension

Start with two clearly different objects and model the comparison. Extend by asking students to sort the same objects in two different ways.

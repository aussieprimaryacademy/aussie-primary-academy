Turn-Taking and Active Listening

Take turns in a discussion and show that you are listening to others. Use this printable in class, tutoring or at home with discussion and supported practice.

Year 1EnglishFree PDF

Learning focus

Take turns in a discussion and show that you are listening to others.

Teacher guidance

Model waiting, looking towards the speaker, responding to the idea and asking a relevant question. Use short partner discussions so students can practise both speaking and listening.

Try it at home

During a family conversation or game, practise waiting for a turn and responding to what another person has said.

Support and extension

Use a talking object or clear turn-taking cue. Extend by asking students to build on a partner's idea with a related comment or question.

About This Grade 1 ELA Worksheet

This Grade 1 worksheet builds foundational writing conventions. Students practise capitalising the first word of a sentence and choosing the correct end mark — period, question mark or exclamation point.

What's Included

  • Capital letter practice at sentence start
  • End punctuation choice tasks
  • Mixed sentence types
  • Teacher answer key

Skills Practiced

  • Capitalising sentence beginnings
  • Using periods, question marks and exclamation points
  • Recognising statement, question and exclamation
  • Proofreading simple sentences

Common Core Connection

Supports CCSS L.1.2.A (capitalise the first word of a sentence) and CCSS L.1.2.B (use end punctuation for sentences).

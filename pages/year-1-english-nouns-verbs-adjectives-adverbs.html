Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives and Adverbs

Notice how different word groups help build meaning in sentences. Use this printable in class, tutoring or at home with discussion and supported practice.

Year 1EnglishFree PDF

Learning focus

Notice how different word groups help build meaning in sentences.

Teacher guidance

Use simple oral examples before asking students to identify words in print. Focus on what a word does in the sentence: naming, action or adding detail. Avoid teaching labels without connecting them to meaning.

Try it at home

Choose an object and describe it, then make up an action for it. Talk about which words name things, show actions or add detail.

Support and extension

Begin with clear examples and colour-coded word cards. Extend by asking students to improve a plain sentence with precise describing or action words.

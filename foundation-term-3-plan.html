Foundation Term 3 Learning Plan

Extend familiar skills through more independent practice, connected reading and writing, data, time and practical science.

How to use this plan

  • Choose one short Maths, one English / Phonics and one Science activity each week.
  • Use physical materials, discussion, reading aloud and adult modelling alongside the linked worksheet.
  • Repeat or adapt activities when a learner needs more practice. This is a flexible practice sequence, not a fixed school program.
  • Curriculum alignment belongs to each individual resource page. Check that page for its descriptor and learning intention; this plan does not add new curriculum codes.

Scope note: Foundation Mathematics here focuses on Foundation-level number, patterns, measurement, shape, position, time and data. Fractions and decimals are not included in this Foundation plan.

Teacher and family guidance

Keep learning active and age-appropriate. Ask the child to explain, point, sort, draw, build, read or describe rather than relying only on written answers. For Science, pair worksheet tasks with safe, supervised observation and hands-on exploration.

This plan covers selected APA Maths, English / Phonics and Science resources. It is not a complete statement of every Australian Curriculum learning area or a substitute for a school’s full teaching program. See the Australian Curriculum for official curriculum information.

Reading skills students can practise

  • Early reading, print concepts, sight words and decodable text
  • Retelling, sequencing, predicting and making connections
  • Finding key information, identifying main ideas and summarising
  • Vocabulary, comparing texts and making inferences
  • Explaining answers using details from a text

Choose activities that match what the student has been taught and the support they need. Curriculum references and skill details are provided on individual resource pages where available.

A Simple 3-Week Warm-Up Plan

No pressure, no long sessions. Three small steps before the first day.

  1. Week 1: Review

    Print one worksheet from the year level just finished. Keep it relaxed: the goal is to remember what they already know, not to test them.

  2. Week 2: Build

    Move to the new year level with one maths and one English or reading sheet. Do 10 to 15 minutes, then stop while it still feels easy.

  3. Week 3: Settle

    Reset the daily rhythm: reading time, a short writing task and an early night. Choose one favourite worksheet to repeat for confidence.

Your Back-to-School Readiness Checklist

  • Print one maths and one English or reading worksheet for the first week.
  • Keep sessions short: 10 to 15 minutes, a few times a week.
  • Read together every day, even if only for 10 minutes.
  • Use the answer page afterwards and talk through any mistakes calmly.
  • Start a simple routine with the free planner, reading log and homework checklist.
  • Stop on a win. Finishing on an easy question builds confidence for day one.

Back-to-School Questions

When should I start preparing for Term 1 2027?

Most Australian schools start Term 1 between late January and early February, and dates vary by state and sector. A relaxed start two to three weeks before school begins is enough. Short, regular sessions of 10 to 15 minutes work better than one long session.

Are these worksheets really free?

Yes. Every worksheet linked on this page is a free printable PDF with no sign-up required, aligned to the Australian Curriculum v9.0.

How do I choose the right year level?

For a gentle review, start with the year level your child has just finished. For a head start, try the year they are about to begin. If a worksheet feels too hard, step back to the previous year.

Do the worksheets include answers?

Yes. Our worksheets include an answer page for parents and teachers, plus a short parent tip so you can support learning without giving the answers away.

Can teachers use these in the classroom?

Yes. Teachers, tutors and homeschool families can print them for classroom or home use. Each worksheet shows its Australian Curriculum code and learning intention.

What are CVC Words? A Simple Guide for Parents

Is your child in Foundation or Year 1 and starting to read 3-letter words like cat, dog and sun? Those are CVC words — the most important first step to reading.

Browse Free CVC Worksheets

What Are CVC Words?

CVC stands for Consonant – Vowel – Consonant. It’s a simple 3-letter word pattern where each letter makes its most common sound.

CVC Words List and Examples

  • -at family: cat, hat, mat, sat, bat
  • -og family: dog, log, fog, jog
  • -in family: pin, bin, tin, win
  • -en family: pen, hen, ten, men
  • -un family: sun, bun, run, fun
  • -ug family: mug, bug, hug, jug

Why Are CVC Words So Important?

1. Blending: Putting sounds together. c – a – t = cat

2. Segmenting: Breaking a word apart. cat = c – a – t

How to Practise at Home (5-Min Ideas)

1. Oral Blending Game: Say “m-a-p” and ask “What word?”
2. Word Families: One family at a time.
3. Find the Sound: What is middle sound in dog?

FAQ

When to start CVC? In Foundation once children know A–Z sounds.

10 easy CVC words? cat, dog, sun, pen, hat, big, map, bed, cup, log.

What comes after CVC? Blends (bl, st) and Digraphs (sh, ch, th).

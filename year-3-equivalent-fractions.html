Equivalent Fractions

Help Year 3 students understand that fractions can look different but still have the same value. This free printable worksheet builds strong fraction sense with clear visual models and practice questions.

Learning Intention

Students will recognise and create simple equivalent fractions using visual models and number lines.

Australian Curriculum

AC9M3N02

Recognise and represent unit fractions including 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5 and 1/10 and their multiples in different ways.

Teacher Notes

Start with concrete materials or drawings (paper folding, fraction bars). Then move to the worksheet. Ask students to explain why two fractions are equal.

Skills Practised

  • Recognising equivalent fractions
  • Using visual models
  • Fraction reasoning
  • Comparing simple fractions

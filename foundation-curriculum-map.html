How to use this curriculum map

This is a quick guide to selected APA resources, not a complete curriculum checklist or a full teaching program. A linked worksheet can support practice with the skill shown, but one worksheet does not cover every part of a content description or achievement standard.

Parents can choose a skill for extra practice. Teachers and homeschool educators can use the codes to help locate a resource while planning learning experiences and checking student understanding.

Foundation Science resources focus on observable external features and grouping plants and animals.

Maths

Selected resources across Number, Algebra, Measurement, Space and Statistics.

AC9MFN02

Subitising

Recognise and name collections of up to five objects without counting each one.

APA worksheet: Subitising: How Many?

AC9MFN03

Counting and comparing collections

Count and compare collections to at least 20, explaining which has more, fewer or the same.

APA worksheet: Counting and Comparing Collections to 20

AC9MFN04

Part-part-whole relationships

Partition and combine collections up to 10 using part-part-whole relationships.

APA worksheet: Part-Part-Whole: Making 10

AC9MFN05

Practical addition and subtraction

Represent practical situations involving addition and subtraction using materials and counting strategies.

APA worksheet: Adding and Taking Away to 10

AC9MFA01

Repeating patterns

Recognise, copy and continue repeating patterns represented in different ways.

APA worksheet: Repeating Patterns

AC9MFSP01

Familiar shapes

Sort, name and create familiar shapes and recognise shapes in everyday objects.

APA worksheet: Shapes: Sort, Name and Create

AC9MFM01

Measurement attributes

Compare length, capacity, mass and duration using direct comparisons.

APA worksheet: Length, Mass, Capacity and Duration

AC9MFST01

Collecting and sorting data

Collect, sort and compare data represented by objects and images in familiar situations.

APA worksheet: Sort, Count and Picture Graph

English

Selected resources for reading, comprehension, phonological awareness, grammar and writing.

AC9EFLY09

Rhyming and phonological awareness

Recognise and generate rhymes, alliteration patterns, syllables and sounds in spoken words.

APA worksheet: Rhyming Words

AC9EFLY11

Letter names and sounds

Recognise upper- and lower-case letters and know the most common sound each letter represents.

APA worksheet: Letter Sounds A to M

AC9EFLY10

Blending and segmenting sounds

Practise orally blending and segmenting single-syllable words and identifying phonemes.

APA worksheet: Blending and Segmenting CVC Words

AC9EFLY10

Beginning phonemes

APA worksheet: Beginning Sounds

AC9EFLY14

High-frequency words

APA worksheet: High-Frequency Sight Words

Decodable reading

APA worksheet: Decodable Sentences

External features of animals

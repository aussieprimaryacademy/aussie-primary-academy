Teacher guidance for the final week of term

  • Invite students to reflect on a skill or achievement they are proud of.
  • Use a memory-book prompt as a short portfolio activity.
  • Give Year 6 students time to list questions and identify trusted adults for secondary school.
  • Review water-safety messages using the resource and school guidance.
  • Choose optional holiday learning that suits each child's year level and energy.

Frequently asked questions

What are end-of-year worksheets?

Optional activities for reflecting on learning, preparing for transitions and discussing relevant safety topics.

Which year levels are covered?

Core end-of-year resources cover Years 1–6. Existing Spring holiday packs are available from Foundation to Year 6.

Can teachers use these resources in class?

Yes. Check each resource page for its learning purpose, year range and classroom guidance.

Where can Year 6 students find transition activities?

Use the Year 6 Secondary School Transition Workbook linked above.

Are holiday learning packs available?

Yes. Existing Spring School Holiday Learning Packs are available; they have not been relabelled as summer packs.

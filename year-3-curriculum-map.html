How to use this curriculum map

This is a quick guide to selected APA resources, not a complete curriculum checklist or a full teaching program. A linked worksheet can support practice with the skill shown, but one worksheet does not cover every part of a content description or achievement standard.

Parents can choose a skill for extra practice. Teachers and homeschool educators can use the codes to help locate a resource while planning learning experiences and checking student understanding.

Maths

Selected resources across Number, Algebra, Measurement, Space and Statistics.

AC9M3N03

Addition and subtraction strategies

Add and subtract two- and three-digit numbers using place-value partitioning and regrouping.

APA worksheet: Addition and Subtraction Strategies

AC9M3A03

Multiplication facts

Recall multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 and connect them to related division facts.

APA worksheet: Multiplication Tables Practice

AC9M3N02

Unit fractions

Recognise and represent unit fractions and their multiples, and combine fractions with the same denominator.

APA worksheet: Unit Fractions and Models

AC9M3M01

Choosing metric units

Identify suitable metric units for everyday items and use familiar measurements to make estimates.

APA worksheet: Choosing Metric Units and Estimating

AC9M3M05

Angles as turns

Identify angles as measures of turn and compare angles with right angles.

APA worksheet: Angles as Turns and Right Angles

AC9M3ST01

Collecting and recording data

Acquire data to answer a question and record it using appropriate methods such as tables.

APA worksheet: Collecting Data

Worksheet differentiation: Support Core Extend

English

Selected resources for reading, comprehension, phonological awareness, grammar and writing.

AC9E3LY05

Inference and comprehension

Use comprehension strategies to build literal and inferred meaning and evaluate texts.

APA worksheet: Inference Skills Reading

AC9E3LY03 · AC9E3LY05

Non-fiction reading

Identify a text's purpose and use comprehension strategies to understand non-fiction information.

APA worksheet: Reading Comprehension: Nonfiction

AC9E3LY05

Main idea and key details

Use comprehension strategies to identify a text's main idea and relevant supporting details.

APA worksheet: Main Idea and Key Details

AC9E3LY05

Cause and effect in reading

Use comprehension strategies to connect information and explain cause-and-effect relationships in a text.

APA worksheet: Cause and Effect Reading

AC9E3LY06

Narrative writing

Plan, create, edit and publish an imaginative text with a clear sequence and relevant details.

APA worksheet: The Mysterious Door: Narrative Writing

Science

Selected resources connected to Science understanding and Science inquiry.

AC9S3U01

Living and non-living things

APA worksheet: Living and Non-Living Things

Plant and animal life cycles

Examine and represent differences between the life cycles of plants and animals.

How to use this curriculum map

This is a quick guide to selected APA resources, not a complete curriculum checklist or a full teaching program. A linked worksheet can support practice with the skill shown, but one worksheet does not cover every part of a content description or achievement standard.

Parents can choose a skill for extra practice. Teachers and homeschool educators can use the codes to help locate a resource while planning learning experiences and checking student understanding.

Maths

Selected resources across Number, Algebra, Measurement, Space and Statistics.

Comparing and ordering fractions

Compare and order fractions with the same or related denominators, including mixed numerals.

APA worksheet: Fractions: Related Denominators

AC9M5N07

Division and remainders

Solve division problems efficiently and interpret remainders in the context of a problem.

APA worksheet: Division with Remainders

AC9M5N09

Budgeting and financial modelling

Use mathematical modelling and efficient operations to solve practical problems, including financial contexts.

APA worksheet: Budgeting Problems

AC9M5N09

Money and financial maths

Model practical money situations, choose operations and explain solutions in context.

APA worksheet: Money and Financial Maths

AC9M5M04

Measuring angles

12-hour and 24-hour time

Compare 12-hour and 24-hour time systems and solve practical conversion problems.

APA worksheet: 12-Hour and 24-Hour Time

English

Selected resources for reading, comprehension, phonological awareness, grammar and writing.

AC9E5LY05

Reading comprehension

Use comprehension strategies to build literal and inferred meaning and evaluate information and ideas.

APA worksheet: Bushfire Preparedness: Reading Comprehension

Reading comprehension

APA worksheet: Ocean Plastic: Reading Comprehension

Complex sentences and clauses

Science

Selected resources connected to Science understanding and Science inquiry.

AC9S5U01

Adaptations and survival

Examine how structural features and behaviours help living things survive in specific habitats.

APA worksheet: Adaptations and Survival

AC9S5U02

Weathering and erosion

Describe how weathering, erosion, transportation and deposition change Earth's surface.

APA worksheet: Weathering, Erosion and Earth's Surface

AC9S5U03

Light, shadows and reflection

APA worksheet: States of Matter

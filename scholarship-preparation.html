Scholarship & Selective Entry Preparation

APA is developing focused preparation resources for students moving from primary to secondary school. The planned programme will help students practise transferable skills in Maths reasoning and Reading, build familiarity with timed tasks and learn from worked explanations.

In development · Not yet available to purchase

What is being planned?

This is an early-stage APA resource direction. No course, workbook or practice test is currently available to buy. Content and release timing will be confirmed before launch.

Planned preparation areas

Maths reasoning

Multi-step problem solving, number reasoning and explaining mathematical thinking.

Reading comprehension

Understanding texts, interpreting information, vocabulary and evidence-based answers.

Timed practice

Structured practice to help students manage time and approach unfamiliar questions calmly.

Worked explanations

Step-by-step reasoning that helps students understand why an answer works.

Important information for families

Scholarship and selective-entry requirements vary between schools, states and providers. APA is not an official examination body and does not claim alignment with one universal scholarship test. Planned materials will focus on general preparation skills and will be clearly described before release.

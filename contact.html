Contact Us

Questions, feedback, error reports or permission requests — we are happy to hear from Australian parents, teachers, tutors and homeschool families.

How to Contact Aussie Primary Academy

The best way to reach us is through our feedback form. This helps us keep a clear record of your message and respond when contact details are provided.

Open Share Feedback Form

What You Can Contact Us About

Common reasons people get in touch:

  • Feedback

    Suggestions & Ideas

    Tell us what resources would help your family, class or tutoring sessions.

  • Errors

    Report a Problem

    Broken links, download issues, or any mistake in a worksheet or page.

  • Permissions

    Permission Requests

    Questions about using our resources beyond personal or classroom use.

Response Time

We review messages as time allows. If you include a contact email in the form, we will reply when we can. We are a small independent project, so replies may take a few days.

Privacy Note

Please do not include sensitive personal information or unnecessary details about a child in the form. Our Privacy Policy explains how information is handled.
